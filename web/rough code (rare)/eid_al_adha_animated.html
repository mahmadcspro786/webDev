About Eid al-Adha

The Festival of Sacrifice commemorates the devotion of Ibrahim (AS). Muslims around the world offer prayers, share meals with loved ones, and extend generosity to those in need — a celebration of faith, gratitude, and community.

🎉

Celebrate Together

On this blessed day, hearts unite across the world. Share joy, give generously, and make those around you feel the warmth of Eid.
